3 PART 3. READING COMPREHENSION (CAN GIO MANGROVE FOREST)

5 câu hỏi
Reading Passage Bấm "Xem Dẫn Chứng" ở câu hỏi để soi ngay đoạn chứa đáp án!

Can Gio, a coastal district in the south of Viet Nam, is home to a large mangrove forest. These trees protect the coast from storms, give shelter to fish, crabs and birds, and help to clean the air. Their roots also hold the soil in place, which stops the riverbanks from being washed away. Unfortunately, large areas of the forest were destroyed several decades ago, partly because of war and partly because people cut down trees for firewood and shrimp ponds. As a result, many animals lost their homes, and local villagers were left without protection when strong waves hit the shore.

Fortunately, the situation has gradually improved. The local authorities have launched a replanting programme, and thousands of volunteers have planted young trees along the riverbanks. Schools also play an important role: every year, students are taken to the forest to learn about its value, and many of them help to plant new trees. Local people, who once cut the trees, now earn their living by guiding visitors. Tourists can ride small boats through the green tunnels of leaves, but they are not allowed to pick flowers or leave rubbish behind. Thanks to these combined efforts, the forest has recovered, and it is now seen as a natural treasure for both people and wildlife.

Question 12. According to paragraph 1, what happened to many animals when parts of the forest were destroyed?

Chính xác: A. They lost their homes.

Dẫn chứng trong đoạn 1: "As a result, many animals lost their homes..." (Kết quả là, nhiều loài động vật bị mất nơi sinh sống).

Question 13. The word “launched” in paragraph 2 is closest in meaning to ________.

Chính xác: D. started

Giải thích: Từ launched (phát động/khởi xướng) đồng nghĩa với started (bắt đầu).

Question 14. The word “them” in paragraph 2 refers to ________.

Chính xác: B. students

Dẫn chứng: "...students are taken to the forest to learn about its value, and many of them help..." -> đại từ "them" thay thế cho "students".

Question 15. According to paragraph 2, which of the following is TRUE?

Chính xác: C. Students go to the forest every year to learn about its value.

Dẫn chứng: "...every year, students are taken to the forest to learn about its value..." là câu duy nhất ĐÚNG.

Question 16. Which of the following can be the best title for the passage?

Chính xác: A. Rebuilding Forest: People Helping Nature Recover

4 PART 4. SENTENCE ARRANGEMENT

4 câu hỏi

Question 17.

a. Hung: Sure, Mum. Should I wash the vegetables first?

b. Mum: Hung, could you help me prepare dinner tonight?

c. Mum: Yes, please. Then cut them into small pieces.

Chính xác: D. b – a – c

Giải thích: Mẹ nhờ Hùng giúp (b) -> Hùng nhận lời và hỏi bước làm tiếp (a) -> Mẹ hướng dẫn chi tiết (c).

Question 18.

a. For example, parents may want their children to come home early, while teenagers want more freedom.

b. Therefore, both sides should listen to each other and try to understand different opinions.

c. Disagreements between parents and teenagers are common in many families.

d. These different wishes often lead to arguments if nobody is willing to compromise.

Chính xác: B. c – a – d – b

Giải thích: Giới thiệu chủ đề bất đồng (c) -> Nêu ví dụ thực tế (a) -> Dẫn đến tranh cãi (d) -> Đưa ra giải pháp khuyên bảo (b).

Question 19. Dear Tom,

a. I hope you are doing well.

b. Each member will collect plastic bottles and cans around the lake and sort them into different bins.

c. Please let me know if you can come.

d. I’m writing to tell you about my school’s new recycling club.

e. We meet at 8 a.m. every Saturday at the school gate.

Chính xác: C. a – d – e – b – c

Giải thích: Lời chào hỏi thăm (a) -> Mục đích viết thư (d) -> Thời gian/địa điểm (e) -> Hoạt động chi tiết (b) -> Lời mời phản hồi (c).

Question 20.

a. However, cutting down trees without replanting can lead to floods and soil erosion.

b. Forests play a vital role in our daily lives.

c. They produce the oxygen we breathe and provide homes for thousands of animals.

d. If we act now, future generations will still be able to enjoy fresh air and green spaces.

e. For this reason, local communities should plant more trees and protect the remaining forests.

Chính xác: A. b – c – a – e – d

Giải thích: Giới thiệu tầm quan trọng của rừng (b) -> Chi tiết lợi ích (c) -> Cảnh báo nguy cơ phá rừng (a) -> Giải pháp bảo vệ (e) -> Tương lai tích cực (d).

10 câu hỏi

A. Rewrite using the word in brackets (2 Questions)

Question 21. My parents plan to repaint the kitchen this summer. (GOING)

Đáp án chuẩn:

My parents are going to repaint the kitchen this summer.

Cấu trúc: plan to V = be going to V (dự định làm gì).

Question 22. I’ve decided to visit my uncle in Hanoi next summer. (GOING)

Đáp án chuẩn:

I am going to visit my uncle in Hanoi next summer.

Cấu trúc: have decided to V = am/is/are going to V.

B. Change sentences into Passive Voice (8 Questions)

Question 23. People collect plastic bottles in this area every week.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: Plastic bottles...

Đáp án chuẩn:

Plastic bottles are collected in this area every week.

Question 24. The workers are cleaning the river bank at the moment.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: The river bank...

Đáp án chuẩn:

The river bank is being cleaned by the workers at the moment.

Question 25. My grandmother made this scarf from recycled wool last winter.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: This scarf...

Đáp án chuẩn:

This scarf was made from recycled wool by my grandmother last winter.

Question 26. The city council has already banned plastic bags.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: Plastic bags...

Đáp án chuẩn:

Plastic bags have already been banned by the city council.

Question 27. Volunteers will plant five hundred trees along the canal next month.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: Five hundred trees...

Đáp án chuẩn:

Five hundred trees will be planted along the canal next month by volunteers.

Question 28. We must keep the classroom clean.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: The classroom...

Đáp án chuẩn:

The classroom must be kept clean.

Question 29. The teacher gave each student a reusable bottle.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: Each student...

Đáp án chuẩn:

Each student was given a reusable bottle by the teacher.

Question 30. The city is going to build a new recycling centre next year.

Gợi ý bắt đầu: A new recycling centre...

Đáp án chuẩn:

A new recycling centre is going to be built next year.
